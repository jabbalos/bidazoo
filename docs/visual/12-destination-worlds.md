# 12 Hub and Destination World Definitions

Status: PROPOSED. Every destination is an original BidaZoo interpretation inspired by recognizable
geography and cultural architecture. None copies a copyrighted or theme-park rendition.

For this visual-production phase, destination art is presentation only: destination choice must not
alter question difficulty, grading, mastery, XP, coins, or academic progression. Future World
Expedition architecture may use destinations for navigation, curriculum grouping, game selection,
story progression, unlocks, or expedition routing. Those product decisions are OPEN and are not
decided here.

**RESOLVED (owner, October 12, 2026): these seven are the canonical first-seven destinations.** The
production environment render (09) is a source of mood, composition, lighting, parallax concepts,
texture, material language, and environmental density only. It does not override this lineup, and
destination identities are not merged silently.

**Cultural and context review is REQUIRED for ALL seven destinations** before production-final
approval. No destination is culturally cleared merely because a visual concept exists (see the
review table below).

## Hub: BidaZoo World Expedition Basecamp

The central expedition headquarters. Click greets the learner here.

| Area | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Expedition headquarters | Timber-and-canvas main lodge on a stone base, with a brass compass sign |
| Map room | Large floor map with pins and route lines; seven destination doors or route tags |
| Gathering plaza | Open community space with woven-shade canopies, long tables, and hanging lanterns |
| Observatory and learning structures | A rounded brass-dome observatory and a low library hall (no turrets) |
| Adventure Academy (optional) | May exist as a lodge, learning facility, classroom or exploration building, or named program area. Not the product title and must not dominate hub branding. |

Hub palette: timber `#8a5a2b`, canvas `#efe3c8`, jungle continuity `#0f3d2e` and `#1f6f4a`, brass
`#c9922e`, lantern `#ffb03a`, sky `#7ec8e3`. Subtle heritage vocabulary appears throughout (see
[11](11-environment-style.md)).

Layout: a radial plaza with seven paths leading to destinations.

## Destinations

Each entry: inspiration, original interpretation, palette (three to five colors plus an accent),
light, three layers, avoid list, and care notes.

### 1. Great Wall Mountain Expedition

- **Inspiration:** Chinese mountain-ridge fortification walls.
- **Original interpretation:** A stone watch-path winding along a misty ridge with beacon platforms
  and stair runs, drawn as a generic ancient ridge wall, not a specific section.
- **Palette:** ridge green `#5c7c6b`, stone `#b9a98a`, mist `#dfe9e4`, sky dawn `#f3d9a8`;
  accent autumn orange `#d9822b`.
- **Light:** early morning haze.
- **Layers:** far misty peaks; mid ridge wall and beacons; near trees and stair railings.
- **Avoid:** pagoda-roof caricature, dragon clichés, costume stereotypes.

### 2. Petra Rose Canyon

- **Inspiration:** Rose-red carved sandstone canyon architecture of Petra.
- **Original interpretation:** A narrow canyon of banded rose stone with an original carved facade
  and a winding path, not a replica of any specific facade.
- **Palette:** rose stone `#d98c78`, `#b8604c`, shadow `#7a3b3a`, sky `#f6d6a8`; accent turquoise
  `#3aa5a0`.
- **Light:** late afternoon glow.
- **Layers:** far canyon walls; mid carved facade; near boulders and a lantern post.
- **Avoid:** movie-set copies, desert-stereotype props, religious symbolism.
- **Readability:** keep the maroon bandana distinguishable from the rose and terracotta stone.

### 3. Andes Cloud Terraces

- **Inspiration:** High Andean stepped terraces and mountain-top stone sites.
- **Original interpretation:** Stepped green terraces climbing a cloud-wrapped peak with stone
  stairs and a lookout, an invented settlement.
- **Palette:** terrace green `#6fa05a`, stone `#9aa08c`, cloud `#f4f6f8`, sky `#6fb4d8`; accent
  warm woven stripe `#e0662a`.
- **Light:** bright high-altitude midday with drifting clouds.
- **Layers:** far peaks and clouds; mid terraces; near grass and stone steps.
- **Avoid:** copying a specific site's layout; stereotyped dress.

### 4. Maya Jungle Observatory

- **Inspiration:** Stepped stone architecture and astronomy tradition of Maya sites in the jungle.
- **Original interpretation:** A moss-covered stepped stone observatory rising above the canopy with
  a sky-viewing platform and a cenote pool below. Ties to the stargazing theme.
- **Palette:** mossy stone `#8d9a7b`, jungle `#1f6f4a`, cenote `#2bb5b0`, dusk sky `#4a5a8c`;
  accent glow gold `#ffc83d`.
- **Light:** golden hour into early dusk.
- **Layers:** far canopy and sky; mid stepped observatory; near vines and ferns.
- **Avoid:** glyph copying, sacrificial or violent imagery, "lost civilization" tropes.
- **Care:** Maya culture is living; review for respect.

### 5. Taj Garden Expedition

- **Inspiration:** Mughal-era formal gardens and reflecting pools with white-marble pavilions.
- **Original interpretation:** A garden axis with a long reflecting channel, clipped hedges, and an
  original white-marble pavilion with a rounded dome, not a replica of the mausoleum.
- **Palette:** marble ivory `#f4efe3`, pool `#5aa9c4`, garden `#4f9a6a`, sunrise sky `#f2c9a0`;
  accent saffron `#e8a33d`.
- **Light:** soft sunrise.
- **Layers:** far pavilion and sky; mid reflecting channel; near hedges and flower beds.
- **Avoid:** depicting the real monument as a landmark replica, play on a burial site, caricature.
- **Care:** the real monument is a mausoleum; keep the tone respectful.

### 6. Nile Discovery Route

- **Inspiration:** The Nile river valley, its boats, and monumental stone architecture.
- **Original interpretation:** A river route with sail boats, papyrus banks, and an invented
  monumental stone gateway, framed as a discovery expedition.
- **Palette:** river `#3b8fa8`, sandstone `#e0c28a`, papyrus `#7fae5a`, sky `#f6e0b0`; accent lapis
  `#2f55a4`.
- **Light:** warm midday with long reflections.
- **Layers:** far dunes and sky; mid river and gateway; near papyrus reeds and a dock.
- **Avoid:** mummy and curse clichés, copying specific temples.

### 7. Philippine Highlands & Lantern Harbor

- **Inspiration:** Terraced rice highlands and a festive lantern-lit coastal harbor.
- **Original interpretation:** Green rice terraces step down toward a bay harbor of stilt and
  bamboo structures. Woven geometry trim, rattan details, and glowing lanterns light the evening.
  This is the strongest explicit heritage expression in the set.
- **Palette:** terrace green `#5fa84a`, bamboo `#c9b45a`, rattan `#b8844a`, harbor teal `#2aa7a1`,
  dusk sky gradient `#5a3d7a` to `#f08a5d`; accent lantern amber `#ffb03a`.
- **Light:** blue-hour dusk with lantern glow.
- **Layers:** far highlands and dusk sky; mid terraces and harbor; near bamboo railings and lanterns.
- **Avoid:** costume stereotypes, copying specific textile or sacred patterns, reducing the culture
  to one prop.
- **Readability:** Dino's teal must stay distinct from the harbor teal; keep a warm gap behind Dino.

important. This package does not claim any destination has been reviewed or cleared. Status of all seven: NOT CLEARED.

## Selection and presentation (PROPOSED)

presentation only. What destination choice may later mean for the product is OPEN. The hub and all
seven destinations use the same UI language ([10](10-ui-component-style.md)).
