# 11 World Expedition Environment Style Guide

Status: PROPOSED. The castle and fantasy-city look in the old reference art is not authoritative.

**Owner decisions applied (October 12, 2026).** The hub is **BidaZoo World Expedition Basecamp**
("Horizon Basecamp" is rejected). Adventure Academy may exist inside it only as a lodge, learning
facility, classroom or exploration building, or named program area, and it must not dominate hub
branding. Production render 09 is used for **mood, composition, lighting, parallax concepts,
texture, material language, and environmental density**; it does **not** override the canonical
seven-destination lineup. Cultural and context review is **REQUIRED** before production-final
approval for all seven real-world-inspired destinations.

## 1. Direction

Global adventure-learning universe. Recognizable-place inspiration, original BidaZoo
interpretation, exploration paths, maps, expedition hubs, discovery, and subtle heritage.

Move away from: generic fantasy kingdoms, Disney-like castle worlds, and princess or fairy-tale
architecture.

## 2. Shape language (PROPOSED)

- Grounded, hand-built structures: timber, stone, canvas, rope, bamboo, brass.
- Horizontal and terraced forms (terraces, ridgelines, river bends), not tall symmetrical spires.
- Rounded or flat roofs, domes only where the real place inspires them, and never as storybook
  turrets.
- Paths that lead the eye: trails, rope bridges, stairs, river routes.
- Simplified shapes with clear silhouettes; detail concentrated near the playfield.

## 3. Depth and layering

Three layers per scene (see [13](13-phaser-asset-spec.md)): far (sky and distant landmark), mid
(landmark and terrain), near (framing foliage or props). Keep the center band clear so characters
and UI read.

## 4. Light and atmosphere

Warm, directional natural light with atmospheric haze for distance. Each destination has one time
of day. No glitter, no sparkle trails, no neon.

## 5. Discovery cues

Each scene hints at something to find: a distant glowing window, a map pin, a flag on a ridge, a
hidden path. In this phase they are decorative and carry no gameplay meaning. Any later use is OPEN.

## 6. Subtle heritage vocabulary

Used across the hub and sparingly elsewhere. Tasteful and abstract, never a costume or caricature.

| Element | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Woven geometry | Diamond and zigzag trim on awnings, panels, and floor tiles |
| Bamboo and rattan | Railings, lamp frames, shade structures, benches |
| Lantern-inspired shapes | Hanging lights, route markers, loading icons |
| Tropical materials | Thatch, palm, shell, warm timber |
| Community gathering | Open plazas, long shared tables, shaded gathering spaces |

The Philippine destination (see [12](12-destination-worlds.md)) is the strongest explicit
expression. The platform as a whole is not exclusively Filipino-themed.

## 6a. Cultural care

- Draw architecture and landscape inspiration, not sacred symbols, religious figures, or costume
  stereotypes.
- Do not dress the characters in cultural costume as a gimmick.
- Original interpretation only. Do not copy theme-park or copyrighted renditions.
- Cultural and context review is REQUIRED before production-final approval for every real-world-inspired destination (China,
  Jordan, Peru, Mesoamerica, India, Egypt, the Philippines) before final art is approved. See
  [12](12-destination-worlds.md).
- A human look-alike review of final art is also required for the flagged character elements.

## 7. Basecamp hub

Defined in [12](12-destination-worlds.md).

## 8. Readability against characters

Backdrops must not camouflage any character: teal Dino, gray-lavender Koala, white-headed Eagle,
maroon-bandana Bronco, small Tarsier. Keep value contrast behind the playfield.

## 9. Ingestion findings (production sheet 09 and 00)

Sources: `09_World_and_Environment_Guide.png` and the environment strip on
`00_Master_Overview.png`. Detail in [INGESTION-REPORT.md](INGESTION-REPORT.md) section 9.

**Agrees with this guide:** grounded, place-inspired worlds; an explicit DON'T panel against giant
royal castles, generic fantasy spires, and theme-park castles; paths, rope bridges, observatories,
and basecamps; materials of wood, canvas, brass, rope, and stone; geometric, place-inspired
patterns; warm, optimistic light.

**Visual pillars on 09:** real-world inspiration, natural wonders, exploration and discovery,
learning through play, warm and inclusive.
