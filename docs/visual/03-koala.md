## Carried from the reference art (CARRIED)

The fedora and jacket do not conflict with the Bible, so they are retained. Do not redesign Koala
only because those details are not locked.

- Large, fluffy round ears; soft gray-lavender fur with a lighter muzzle.
- Large dark nose; round black-framed glasses.
- Dark fedora-style hat and a dark jacket.
- A notebook or book held in both paws.

## Current transitional code (for reference)

`drawKoala`: lavender head and ears, round glasses, a purple cardigan with a cream collar, and a red
book. No hat, no jacket, no backpack. The production art follows the reference (hat and jacket),
not the placeholder cardigan.

## Production notes (PROPOSED)

- **Silhouette signature.** Wide round ears plus a brimmed hat plus a seated, rounded body. The
  hat brim must not be confused with Bronco's hat brim: Koala's hat is a softer, lower fedora with
  a dark band, while Bronco's is a taller, wider-brimmed hat.
- **No backpack (LOCKED).** Nothing on the Koala may read as a backpack. If an accessory visually
  reads as a backpack, reject it.
- **Other academic accessories are OPEN** unless an approved rendering establishes them. The book
  or notebook is CARRIED from the reference; anything else needs to come from an approved
  rendering or a review decision.
- **Face.** Calm, thoughtful, kind. Eyes visible behind lenses at all sizes.
- **Posture.** Seated or leaning, relaxed, slower than the others. `thinking` suits Koala best.

## Must not

- Have a backpack in any state.
- Lose the gray-lavender fur color to a warm gray or a blue.
- Let the hat or glasses hide the eyes at small sizes.

## OPEN

Child-facing name beyond "Koala", voice or sound, animation set, alternate outfits.
