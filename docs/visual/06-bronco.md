## Carried from the reference art (CARRIED)

The cowboy hat is not prohibited merely because the Bible does not mention it. Retain established
traits unless they conflict with a locked rule.

- Chestnut-brown coat with a dark windswept mane and feathered lower legs.
- Cowboy-style hat with a star emblem; a vest or harness with a star motif.
- A bandana at the neck. In the reference it reads as red.
- Warm, upbeat, determined face.

## Current transitional code (for reference)

maroon bandana (`0x8e1b30`) with cream dots. No hat in the placeholder.

## The bandana color (RESOLVED)

The Bible locks the bandana as **maroon**, and the owner confirmed it on October 12, 2026. The
crimson-red look in the current production render and on the reference board (sampled near
`#c42223`, PROVISIONAL) is **not authoritative**. The semantic rule is MAROON. No exact hex is
locked. `#8e1b30` is only the current code value and a provisional reference. The future corrected
production render must visibly read as maroon at all sizes.

## Production notes (PROPOSED)

- **Silhouette signature.** Long vertical face, tall ears under a wide hat brim, and a flowing
  mane. At 48 px, the hat brim plus the long face is the identifier.
- **Hat.** Tall crown, wide brim, tilted slightly. Distinct from Koala's low soft fedora.
- **Star motif and hat band.** The star and the blue hat band may stay; common symbols are not
  removed merely because they are common. A human visual-originality review is RETAINED for the
  star, the hat-band treatment, and any element that could resemble existing mascot or team
  branding. It judges total composition, shape, proportions, and treatment.
- **Bandana.** Always visible and always maroon, tied at the neck, with a small trim detail.
- **Athletic build.** Strong shoulders and a forward-leaning stance. Energy comes from the pose.

## Must not

- Drop the maroon bandana in any state, or shift it toward bright red.
- Copy a real team, rodeo, or school mark.
- Let the hat hide the eyes or ears.

## OPEN

Child-facing name beyond "Bronco", final star form, voice or sound, animation set, alternate outfits.
