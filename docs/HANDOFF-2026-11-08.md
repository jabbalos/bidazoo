## 4. What happened on November 7 to 8 (including the failures)

Starting point: the November 7 handoff asked for an official Studio validation of `b2e5ef5` (the merged
painted look pass), which had only had sandbox checks ("Playwright subsets").

1. **Validator stopped: port 3001 in use** (`.validation-logs\20261107-165935`). Ports were hard-coded in
   8 files. Owner decision: make them configurable rather than stop the other process. Done in `0271755`.
2. **Validator stopped again** (`20261107-170102`): the new port check reported free ports as busy. Cause:
   PowerShell `return ,$list` on an empty list yields a one-element array. Fixed in `61e9a8d`.
3. **FAIL 227/276** (`20261107-170102`, same folder after the fix): 48 failures, all `cast-lab.spec.ts`
   (12 tests x 4 projects): the lab tests asserted the original production art keys and placement contract;
   the painted pass shows painted expressions in the lab. 1 flaky in `play.spec.ts` (recorder race on the
   last question). Owner decision: tests follow the product (no game code change). Fixed in `2a5695d`;
   verified in the sandbox (40/40 on two projects) before pushing.
4. **FAIL 275/276** (`20261107-184133`): `basecamp.spec.ts` "a viewport change keeps the buddy where it was
   and on screen" on tablet 820x1180: after a rotation-like resize the buddy's right edge was 967 px in a
   944 px view. Cause: the scene kept the buddy's pixel x across a rebuild while the painted world's pixel
   width scales with view height, and the painted manifest's walk limits (3% of the picture) were smaller
   than half the buddy's body. First fix attempt (clamp only) was wrong: it moved the buddy's pixel x and
   would have failed the test's next assertion. Final fix (`390e0b4`): walk limits pulled in by half the
   body width; a rebuild keeps the buddy's fraction of the walkable span; the test compares that fraction.
   Verified in the sandbox: `basecamp.spec.ts` 28/28 on four projects (two sandbox-only timeouts of an
   unrelated test passed with a longer budget).
5. **PASS** (`20261107-220423`). Docs committed. Deployed by the owner with `deploy-390e0b4.bat`.

Time cost: four Studio validator runs (two aborted early, two full) and roughly nine hours end to end.
